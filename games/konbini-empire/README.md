# Konbini Empire

Run a corner konbini one day at a time, shown as an isometric diorama: an orthographic 45-degree camera over a cutaway shop on a town lot (street, trees, lamps), with pixel-art billboard sprites. Each morning you see the weather and the day's event, stock six shelves at today's wholesale prices and set price tags. Open the shop and watch customers browse, buy or leave asking for things you don't carry. Food, milk, fish, flowers and papers spoil at close. Rent is due every Sunday and rises 10% a week; miss it and the run ends. After each day you draft one of three upgrades (staff, self-checkout, fryer, truck, lucky cat...). Renovate into a supermarket (12 shelves, fresh food), open branches, and build the flagship hypermarket to win.

ARRANGE mode lets you move shelf units and the counter around a floor grid and buy decorations (plants, sakura tree, gachapon, vending machine, ATM, aquarium...). Each decoration adds appeal (+2% customers per point) or a specific effect; customers path around whatever you place, and a spot that would wall off a shelf or the till shows red. Renovating keeps decorations that still fit.

**Run:** open `project.godot` in Godot 4.6 and press Play.

**Controls:** mouse or touch. `Space`/`Enter` opens the shop, skips the day replay and continues; `Tab` switches store; `G` opens Expand; `E` toggles Arrange (click an object to pick it up, click a tile to drop it, right-click or `Esc` cancels); `1`-`4` picks an upgrade; `Esc` closes a dialog. The seed button starts a new run, optionally with a seed.

**Check:** `godot --headless --path games/konbini-empire --script res://tests/balance.gd` plays 120 seeded runs with a greedy bot, asserts the supermarket and flagship are reachable, that runs can go bankrupt, that a seed replays identically, and that floor-plan rules hold (overlaps and walled-in shelves refused, decor sells and survives a refit).

**Art:** sprites and backgrounds generated by Codex image_gen on a magenta key, cut and downsampled into `art/`. Font: Kenney Pixel (CC0).
