# Konbini Empire

Run a corner konbini one day at a time. Each morning you see the weather and the day's event, stock six shelves at today's wholesale prices and set price tags. Open the shop and watch customers browse, buy or leave asking for things you don't carry. Food, milk, fish, flowers and papers spoil at close. Rent is due every Sunday and rises 10% a week; miss it and the run ends. After each day you draft one of three upgrades (staff, self-checkout, fryer, truck, lucky cat...). Renovate into a supermarket (12 shelves, fresh food), open branches, and build the flagship hypermarket to win.

**Run:** open `project.godot` in Godot 4.6 and press Play.

**Controls:** mouse or touch. `Space`/`Enter` opens the shop, skips the day replay and continues; `Tab` switches store; `G` opens Expand; `1`-`4` picks an upgrade; `Esc` closes a dialog. The seed button starts a new run, optionally with a seed.

**Check:** `godot --headless --path games/konbini-empire --script res://tests/balance.gd` plays 120 seeded runs with a greedy bot, asserts the supermarket and flagship are reachable, that runs can go bankrupt, and that a seed replays identically.

**Art:** sprites and backgrounds generated by Codex image_gen on a magenta key, cut and downsampled into `art/`. Font: Kenney Pixel (CC0).
