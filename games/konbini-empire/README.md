# Konbini Empire

Run a konbini in Tokyo against rival chains, one day at a time. The city is 48 real neighbourhoods laid out roughly where they are: Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Ueno and Tokyo Station as Yamanote Line hubs, Ginza and Aoyama luxury, Roppongi and Ebisu nightlife, Akihabara otaku, Asakusa and Odaiba tourists, Sugamo grandmothers, Tsukiji early workers, the Imperial Palace in the middle. Each sends out its own crowd at its own hours. Streets step and jog between blocks of different sizes, the Sumida River runs down the east side under bridges, the bay cuts a ragged coast in the south-east, and Yamanote trains run on an elevated loop between the real stations. Landmarks (Tokyo Tower, Skytree, Kaminarimon, Shibuya crossing and more) are Codex pixel art. Every shopper picks a store by distance, prices, reputation and decor, or stays home, so your shoppers are the ones you win from Sora Store, Maru Mart, Midori 24 and Yoru Mart. Rivals open stores where the crowds are (aggressive ones right next to yours), start price wars when anyone opens nearby, close stores that lose money, borrow to expand and sometimes go bankrupt. Open branches on free lots or buy out rival stores from the city map (CITY or M). Each shop is shown as an isometric street block: an orthographic 45-degree camera over a cutaway shop between neighbouring buildings, with walkers on both sidewalks, two lanes of traffic and a side car park. Customers peel off the sidewalk into your shop; the street gets busier at rush hour. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Each morning you see the weather and the day's event, stock six shelves at today's wholesale prices and set price tags. Open the shop and watch customers browse, buy or leave asking for things you don't carry. Food, milk, fish, flowers and papers spoil at close. Rent is due every Sunday and rises 10% a week; miss it and the run ends. After each day you draft one of three upgrades (staff, self-checkout, fryer, truck, lucky cat...). Renovate into a supermarket (12 shelves, fresh food), open branches, and build the flagship hypermarket to win.

ARRANGE mode lets you move shelf units and the counter around a floor grid and buy decorations (plants, sakura tree, gachapon, vending machine, ATM, aquarium...). Each decoration adds appeal (+2% customers per point) or a specific effect; customers path around whatever you place, and a spot that would wall off a shelf or the till shows red. Renovating keeps decorations that still fit.

**Run:** open `project.godot` in Godot 4.6 and press Play.

**Controls:** mouse or touch. `Space`/`Enter` opens the shop, skips the day replay and continues; `Tab` switches store; `M` toggles the city map (click a lot for details, a free lot to open a branch, a rival store to buy it out); `G` opens Expand; `E` toggles Arrange (click an object to pick it up, click a tile to drop it, right-click or `Esc` cancels); `1`-`4` picks an upgrade; `Esc` closes a dialog. The seed button starts a new run, optionally with a seed.

**Check:** `godot --headless --path games/konbini-empire --script res://tests/balance.gd` plays 120 seeded runs with a greedy bot, asserts the supermarket and flagship are reachable, that runs can go bankrupt, that a seed replays identically, that every shopper the city generates either picks a store or stays home, that rivals open stores and chains go bankrupt across runs, and that floor-plan rules hold (overlaps and walled-in shelves refused, decor sells and survives a refit).

**Art:** sprites and backgrounds generated by Codex image_gen on a magenta key, cut and downsampled into `art/`. Font: Noto Sans (SIL Open Font License).
