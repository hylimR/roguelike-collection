# Konbini Empire

Run a konbini in Tokyo against rival chains, one day at a time. The city is 48 real neighbourhoods laid out roughly where they are: Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Ueno and Tokyo Station as Yamanote Line hubs, Ginza and Aoyama luxury, Roppongi and Ebisu nightlife, Akihabara otaku, Asakusa and Odaiba tourists, Sugamo grandmothers, Tsukiji early workers, the Imperial Palace in the middle. Each sends out its own crowd at its own hours. The city is drawn like a city builder: blocks split again and again so side streets meet in T-junctions, curved avenues (Meiji-dori, Yasukuni-dori, Chuo-dori...) cut across them, the elevated Shuto expressway rings the palace (C1) and runs out along the radial avenues with ramps down to the street, the Rainbow Bridge crosses to Odaiba, and Yamanote trains circle on their viaduct. Every block is packed with 3D buildings in a pixel facade shader: glass towers round the station hubs, neon buildings in the nightlife districts, wooden machiya in the old town, houses with gable roofs in the suburbs, warehouses by the markets. The Sumida runs under bridges, the bay holds Tsukishima, Toyosu and Odaiba, and parks (the palace and its moat, Ueno, Yoyogi, Shinjuku Gyoen) are full of trees. Buildings line the curved avenues too, turned to face them, and everything casts a shadow away from the sun. Landmarks (Tokyo Tower, Skytree, Kaminarimon, Shibuya crossing and more) are Codex pixel art; traffic keeps left, including up on the expressway, and cars come down the off-ramps onto the avenues and climb the on-ramps. As the day replay runs into the night, office and flat windows light up and the neon signs glow. Every shopper picks a store by distance, prices, reputation and decor, or stays home, so your shoppers are the ones you win from Sora Store, Maru Mart, Midori 24 and Yoru Mart. Rivals open stores where the crowds are (aggressive ones right next to yours), start price wars when anyone opens nearby, close stores that lose money, borrow to expand and sometimes go bankrupt. Open branches on free lots or buy out rival stores from the city map (CITY or M). Each shop is shown on its own street: an orthographic 45-degree camera over a cutaway shop between its neighbours, with the buildings, trees and expressway in front of it cut away. Customers peel off the sidewalk into your shop; the street gets busier at rush hour. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag to turn the city map (street names and expressway route shields appear close up). Each morning you see the weather and the day's event, stock six shelves at today's wholesale prices and set price tags. Open the shop and watch customers browse, buy or leave asking for things you don't carry. Food, milk, fish, flowers and papers spoil at close. Rent is due every Sunday and rises 10% a week; miss it and the run ends. After each day you draft one of three upgrades (staff, self-checkout, fryer, truck, lucky cat...). Renovate into a supermarket (12 shelves, fresh food), open branches, and build the flagship hypermarket to win.

ARRANGE mode lets you move shelf units and the counter around a floor grid and buy decorations (plants, sakura tree, gachapon, vending machine, ATM, aquarium...). Each decoration adds appeal (+2% customers per point) or a specific effect; customers path around whatever you place, and a spot that would wall off a shelf or the till shows red. Renovating keeps decorations that still fit.

**Run:** open `project.godot` in Godot 4.6 and press Play.

**Controls:** mouse or touch. `Space`/`Enter` opens the shop, skips the day replay and continues; `Tab` switches store; `M` toggles the city map (click a lot for details, a free lot to open a branch, a rival store to buy it out); `G` opens Expand; `E` toggles Arrange (click an object to pick it up, click a tile to drop it, right-click or `Esc` cancels); `1`-`4` picks an upgrade; `Esc` closes a dialog. The seed button starts a new run, optionally with a seed.

**Check:** `godot --headless --path games/konbini-empire --script res://tests/balance.gd` plays 120 seeded runs with a greedy bot, asserts the supermarket and flagship are reachable, that runs can go bankrupt, that a seed replays identically, that every shopper the city generates either picks a store or stays home, that rivals open stores and chains go bankrupt across runs, and that floor-plan rules hold (overlaps and walled-in shelves refused, decor sells and survives a refit).

**Code:** `city.gd` lays Tokyo out (districts, water, parks, streets, avenues, expressway, lots, buildings; built once and cached), `sim.gd` holds the rules, `store_view.gd` draws the city and the shop, `main.gd` the UI.

**Art:** sprites and backgrounds generated by Codex image_gen on a magenta key, cut and downsampled into `art/`; building facades are drawn in code. Font: Noto Sans (SIL Open Font License).
